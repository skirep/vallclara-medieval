# Fotogrames dibuixats de caminar

Vuit fulls originals generats amb imagegen, 6 fotogrames × 4 orientacions per figura: 192 dibuixos de caminar. Els PNG originals es conserven i es reempaqueten al navegador amb el manifest walk-layouts.js.

## tunic-man

Use case: historical-scene. Production spritesheet for a medieval 2.5D walking character. Subject: adult male peasant, short brown hair and beard, moss green knee-length tunic, brown belt, brown leggings and boots. ONE transparent square image EXACTLY SIX COLUMNS and FOUR ROWS, 24 separate full-body sprites. The same individual and outfit in every cell. ROW 1 all six frames FRONT view toward camera. ROW 2 all six frames LEFT SIDE profile walking toward right edge of image. ROW 3 all six frames BACK view away from camera. ROW 4 all six frames RIGHT SIDE profile walking toward left edge. Each row is a seamless SIX-FRAME WALK CYCLE in order: 1 left foot forward right foot behind, 2 weight on left planted foot right heel lifting, 3 right knee raised passing stationary left leg, 4 right foot forward left foot behind, 5 weight on right planted foot left heel lifting, 6 left knee raised passing stationary right leg. Arms swing opposite legs, alternating clearly different drawn leg and arm positions in every successive cell. Preserve same face, clothes, body scale and consistent baseline; subtle vertical weight shift, no jumping or running. Each character fully visible with 15% transparent padding and even alignment, no overlap between cells. Hand-painted detailed pixel art, crisp textured fabrics and warm subdued colors, premium modern retro Doom-like FPS sprites, orthographic camera at chest height, non-isometric. 14th-century Catalan village. No grid, no captions, no floor, no props, no shadow outside character, no background, actual transparent alpha. All 24 figures must be present.
